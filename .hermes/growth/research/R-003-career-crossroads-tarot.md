# R-003 — Career Crossroads Tarot Entry Experience

Status: RESEARCH

## Target query family

- career tarot reading
- career tarot spread
- tarot career crossroads
- should I quit my job tarot

## User intent

A visitor is facing a work decision and wants a structured reflective reading rather than a generic daily tarot draw.

## Evidence

Current results include focused career-crossroads spreads from Spiritclick, TarotAxis, Eldrin, Tarot Spreads, ArcanaPath, and others. The strongest pages frame the reading as decision clarification, not guaranteed prediction, and include practical next-step questions.

## Existing competitors

- Spiritclick — “Should I Quit My Job?” crossroads spread
- TarotAxis — career direction and work spreads
- Eldrin — career crossroads with practical next-step framing
- Tarot Spreads — six-card career crossroads spread
- ArcanaPath — career path spread with live reading CTA

## Why CSG may compete

CSG contains Career Crossroads Tarot in product configuration, tarot draw/interpretation infrastructure, and a Vocation & Wealth Map product. The product fit is direct, but the relevant products are currently hidden and must not be marketed as live without production verification.

## Proposed wedge

A focused free career-decision spread with explicit question prompts, practical reflection output, and responsible language. Avoid a thin generic AI reading.

## Conversion hypothesis

Free career decision spread → save/account → relevant paid tarot product or Vocation & Wealth Map when confirmed live.

## Validation

Measure impressions, completion, saves, repeat use, product clicks, and qualitative usefulness. Test one focused entry experience before a larger career content cluster.

## Unknowns

- Current production exposure of Career Crossroads Tarot
- Conversion rate from free career reading to paid product
- Whether CSG's existing tarot experience can support the required practical framing without new engine work
