# R-004 — Moon Sign and Birth Phase Calculator

Status: RESEARCH

## Target query family

- moon sign calculator
- moon sign by birth date/time/place
- moon phase you were born under

## User intent

A visitor wants a fast personalized Moon sign, degree, and birth-phase result from birth data.

## Evidence

The current SERP contains numerous calculators and astrology sites offering Moon sign, degree, house, and sometimes birth-phase results, including Augurine, Palaura, NexaCurrent, natalcalculator.com, Roseglyph, Regu, Cafe Astrology, and J-Kit.

## Existing competitors

The category is crowded with established astrology calculators and reference sites.

## Why CSG may compete

CSG has Moon-phase calculation, ephemeris data, natal chart calculation, and a free Birth Chart experience.

## Proposed wedge

A compact Moon result that shows sign, degree, phase, a grounded explanation, and a direct path into the fuller free chart and Love Blueprint.

## Conversion hypothesis

Moon result → free chart → account → Love Blueprint.

## Validation

Measure calculator completion, chart continuation, account creation, and Love Blueprint clicks.

## Risks / unknowns

- Crowded SERP and incumbent authority
- Likely low commercial intent as a standalone surface
- Potential duplication with the existing Birth Chart flow
- Search volume and CSG ranking feasibility are UNKNOWN
