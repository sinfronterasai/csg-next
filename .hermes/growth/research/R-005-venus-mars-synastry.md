# R-005 — Venus-Mars Synastry Aspect Checker

Status: RESEARCH

## Target query family

- Venus Mars synastry calculator
- Venus Mars compatibility
- Venus conjunct Mars synastry

## User intent

Two people want to know whether their charts contain Venus-Mars contacts, which aspects/orbs are present, and how to interpret the relationship dynamic.

## Evidence

The current SERP contains full synastry calculators and exact-aspect tools from AstroHot, The Astro Academy, SoulmateChart, and other astrology sites. These products calculate both charts, aspects, orb, and directional context.

## Existing competitors

- AstroHot — full synastry calculator
- The Astro Academy — Venus/Mars compatibility calculator
- SoulmateChart — Venus-Mars tool
- Exact-aspect interpretation pages

## Why CSG may compete

CSG has compatibility/chart engines, report facts, relationship routes/pipeline support, and Love Blueprint at $39. A narrow fact-first checker could be a smaller entry point than a full synastry report.

## Proposed wedge

Collect two birth datasets, isolate Venus-Mars contacts with exact aspect/orb and direction, explain the symbolism without invented compatibility scores, and offer Love Blueprint.

## Conversion hypothesis

Aspect result → save/share → Love Blueprint.

## Validation

Measure two-chart completion, result visibility, shares, account creation, and Love Blueprint clicks.

## Risks / unknowns

- Crowded SERP
- Birth-time friction and privacy concerns
- Need to ensure deterministic aspect/orb facts are correct
- Search volume and ranking feasibility are UNKNOWN
- Do not claim astrology proves relationship outcomes
