# R-006 — Exact Personalized Transit Window on the Existing Transit Surface

Status: RESEARCH

## Opportunity

Turn one existing transit-intent surface into a narrow, fact-first acquisition wedge for queries such as “when is Saturn square my Moon,” “is Saturn opposite my Sun,” and “exact transit dates to my natal chart.”

## User intent

The visitor wants to know whether a named transit is active for them, the exact hit/window dates, and what to do next—not a generic transit definition.

## Evidence

Current results include generic transit-chart tools such as Transits.io and Ascendant Chart. They demonstrate established demand for chart-specific transit lookup, but the visible category is mostly broad calculators rather than a transparent, named-transit window result.

Search volume: UNKNOWN. Current CSG Search Console performance: UNKNOWN.

## Competitor gap / CSG advantage

CSG already has deterministic ephemeris, natal chart, transit, yearly-transit, and known-time policy code. The wedge is transparent exact windows with explicit data-quality limits and no invented astronomical facts, delivered through an existing transit surface rather than a mass page farm.

## Cheaper validation experiment

Use one existing transit route and one named aspect only: Saturn square natal Moon. Add a focused explanatory module and CTA without creating a new page cluster. Run for 30 days.

Baseline: current organic entrances and transit-result completion for the existing route.
Target: UNKNOWN until baseline is collected; pre-register a 20% improvement in result completion or CTA click-through as the pass threshold.
Kill: fewer than 50 qualified organic entrances or no measurable interaction after 30 days.

## Conversion

Transit result → account/save → Yearly Transit Forecast when production availability is verified; otherwise free chart/account retention.

## Scope hypothesis

One reusable named-transit result component, one data contract, one analytics funnel, and one CTA. No mass generation.
