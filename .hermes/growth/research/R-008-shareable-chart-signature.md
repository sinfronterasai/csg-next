# R-008 — Shareable Birth-Chart Signature Card

Status: RESEARCH

## Opportunity

Add a share-ready result card to the existing free Birth Chart: Moon sign, rising sign when birth time is known, dominant element/modality, and one grounded personalized sentence with a CSG destination URL.

## User intent

A visitor wants a compact identity result they can understand and share, then return to for a fuller chart or relationship product.

## Evidence

CSG’s acquisition skill explicitly prioritizes shareable Big Three, dominant element/modality, notable placements, and compatibility highlights. The repository already has natal chart calculation, profile/chart views, deterministic facts, and shareable result candidates. This is a social-discovery experiment, not a claim that a new SEO cluster will rank.

Current social share rate: UNKNOWN.

## Competitor gap / CSG advantage

The differentiation is not a generic quote card. The card is generated from CSG’s verified chart facts and links back to a deterministic result page. It can include an explicit “calculated from your birth data” provenance cue and suppress time-dependent fields when birth time is unknown.

## Cheaper validation experiment

Add a share-card export CTA to the existing chart result using one card layout and one share destination. No new SEO pages. Test with existing chart traffic for 30 days.

Baseline: current chart completion and share events, to be measured during a 7-day instrumentation period.
Target: 5% share/export rate among completed charts and 10% return/referral visits from shared URLs.
Kill: under 1% share/export after 30 days or no measurable return visits.

## Conversion

Free chart → signature card/share → return visit or account → Love Blueprint when the relationship intent is present.

## Scope hypothesis

One deterministic data-to-card serializer, one visual card, one share URL, analytics events, and unknown-time safeguards. No new report engine.
