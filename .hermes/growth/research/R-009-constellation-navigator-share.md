# R-009 — Birth-Chart Constellation Navigator Share Surface

Status: RESEARCH

## Opportunity

Use the existing constellation navigator as a visual acquisition surface: after chart calculation, show the user’s natal constellation/sky relationship with a shareable visual and a path back to the full chart.

## User intent

A visitor wants to see a visual representation of their chart or constellation relationship, not only read a table of placements.

## Evidence

The repository contains constellation star catalog, natal constellation, coordinates, and a constellation navigator UI. This is an existing product capability that can provide interactive differentiation. Search demand for “birth chart constellation” and “constellation astrology calculator” is UNKNOWN and must not be invented.

## Competitor gap / CSG advantage

Many astrology results are text- and wheel-centric. CSG can provide an interactive visual layer using its existing constellation data. The advantage is experiential and shareable, not an unverified claim of a large SEO opportunity.

## Cheaper validation experiment

Instrument and promote the existing navigator to current chart users with one share CTA and one short explanatory entry point. No new indexable cluster. Run a 30-day cohort test.

Baseline: navigator starts/completions and share events before promotion.
Target: 15% of chart completers open the navigator, 5% export/share, and 10% of shared visitors return.
Kill: fewer than 5% navigator opens or zero attributable return visits.

## Conversion

Free chart → navigator → share/return → account → Love Blueprint or another verified relevant product.

## Scope hypothesis

One entry module, instrumentation, share URL/readback, and accessibility/performance pass. No new astronomical claims or report product.
