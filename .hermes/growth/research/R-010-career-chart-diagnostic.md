# R-010 — Privacy-First Career Chart Diagnostic

Status: RESEARCH

## Opportunity

Create a narrowly scoped career diagnostic from CSG’s existing natal engine: public role/MC, daily work, and money themes, with explicit known-time requirements and a waitlist/interest CTA until the Vocation & Wealth Map product is verified for public sale.

## User intent

A visitor wants chart-based career direction and practical themes from exact birth data, not a generic Sun-sign career article.

## Evidence

Current results include DesiUtils, Astromitra, Kaalveda, Vedic Rishi, AstroMool, and ZODIA career calculators. These tools show clear user intent for birth-data career analysis, but the category is competitive and many competitors are Vedic-specific. CSG’s current product configuration includes a hidden Vocation & Wealth Map and the repository contains career-window/report infrastructure.

## Competitor gap / CSG advantage

CSG can offer a Western/tropical, privacy-forward, deterministic fact layer with transparent “known birth time required” behavior, then a narrative interpretation. The first experiment must not promise a paid product that is not publicly verified.

## Cheaper validation experiment

Run a single non-indexed landing/interactive prototype to existing chart traffic and a small opt-in audience. Collect completion, useful/not-useful feedback, and waitlist interest before exposing a public paid route.

Baseline: no career-diagnostic completion or waitlist baseline; establish a 7-day instrumentation baseline.
Target: 25% completion among starters, 15% useful-rating submissions, and 5% qualified waitlist/paid-interest actions.
Kill: under 10% completion or zero qualified interest after 50 starts.

## Conversion

Career diagnostic → account/waitlist → Vocation & Wealth Map only after production status, checkout, and report path are verified.

## Scope hypothesis

One fact pack for career indicators, one short result, explicit uncertainty rules, feedback/waitlist events, and no public product claim until the paid path is live.
