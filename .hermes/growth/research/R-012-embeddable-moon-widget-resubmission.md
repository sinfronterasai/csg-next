# R-012 — Embeddable Current Moon Phase Widget (Resubmission)

Status: RESEARCH

Opportunity: A small deterministic Moon-phase embed for external publishers, newsletters, and spiritual communities.

Evidence and gap: Time.now and similar current-Moon widgets validate the format. CSG’s deterministic Moon-phase calculation can support an accurate, tasteful, versioned embed with a useful current-sky destination. This competes through distribution and referrals, not a broad SERP.

Cheapest experiment: static demo embed plus docs page, sent to 20 preselected public sites in astrology, astronomy, spiritual newsletters, and creator communities. A completed outreach attempt means a documented contact to a site with a public audience and relevant editorial topic. No signed endpoint or marketplace is built in the first test.

Primary metric: qualified install. Definition: a non-CSG public site has the demo visibly live for at least 7 days with the current version and attribution link intact.
Secondary: written interest, embed loads, attribution-link CTR, CSG destination sessions, chart starts.
Baseline: zero qualified installs.
Success: 3 qualified installs or 5 written expressions of interest in 30 days, with at least 1 attributable CSG referral session.
Kill: fewer than 1 qualified install, zero written interest, or zero attributable referral sessions after 30 days.

Attribution: signed demo ID, 30-day referral window, exclude CSG-owned sites and bot traffic; no personal data collection.

Conversion: embed → current Moon page → free chart/account → verified relevant product.

Scope if validated: signed endpoint, responsive/accessibility behavior, CSP/CORS rules, versioned calculation contract, docs, analytics. No manipulative link scheme.
