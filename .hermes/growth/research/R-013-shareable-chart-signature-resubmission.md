# R-013 — Shareable Birth-Chart Signature Card (Resubmission)

Status: RESEARCH

Opportunity: Add a share/export card to completed free Birth Charts using only verified, non-identifying chart facts.

Evidence and gap: CSG already calculates natal facts and the growth strategy prioritizes shareable Big Three, dominant element/modality, and notable placements. The differentiator is deterministic provenance plus unknown-time suppression, not a generic quote image.

Cheapest experiment: 30-day treatment/control test on existing chart results after 7-day baseline instrumentation. Treatment receives one share-card CTA and one layout; control remains unchanged. No new indexable pages.

Public schema: sign/placement labels, dominant element/modality, safe display text, calculation/version cue. Exclude DOB, exact birth time, birthplace, account ID, raw coordinates, and free-text personal inputs by default. Unknown-time fields are omitted. Public URL uses an opaque random ID, explicit user confirmation, noindex, revocation, deletion, rate limiting, and no public search listing.

Primary metric: successful share/export / eligible completed charts in treatment.
Secondary: share URL opens, attributable return within 30 days, account creation, Love Blueprint CTA clicks, broken-link rate, fact-validation errors.
Baseline: 7-day completed-chart volume and current return rate.
Success: 5% successful share/export with at least 100 eligible treatment completions, 10% of unique shared URL visitors return within 30 days, and zero privacy/fact incidents.
Kill: under 1% share/export, zero attributable returns, any privacy incident, fact error, or chart-completion regression over 1 percentage point.

Conversion: chart → card → return/account; Love Blueprint CTA only after explicit relationship-intent interaction.

Scope: deterministic serializer, one visual card, opaque/revocable URL, events, privacy/readback tests. No viral growth claims before cohort data.
