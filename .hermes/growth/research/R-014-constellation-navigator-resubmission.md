# R-014 — Birth-Chart Constellation Navigator Share Surface (Resubmission)

Status: RESEARCH

Opportunity: Promote the existing constellation navigator to completed-chart users as a visual/shareable continuation.

Evidence and gap: Repository contains constellation catalog, natal constellation, coordinates, and navigator UI. Search demand remains UNKNOWN, so this is a product-led/social test rather than an SEO claim. The visual experience is the differentiator.

Cheapest experiment: 30-day 50/50 control/treatment test among completed-chart users. Treatment sees one navigator entry module and one explicit share/export CTA; control sees current chart result. No new indexable cluster.

Denominators and events: eligible completed chart; module impression/click; navigator started/completed; share initiated/succeeded; public URL opened; return within 30 days; account creation; verified product click. A shared visitor is unique by privacy-safe session attribution and deduplicated for the window. Bots, owners, and repeated opens are excluded.

Public share behavior: explicit confirmation, opaque URL, no birth inputs or account identifiers, noindex, revocation, rate limiting, and no unsupported astronomical claims.

Baseline: 7-day navigator starts/completions/shares and chart-result performance before treatment.
Success: treatment navigator-open rate ≥15% of eligible chart completers, share/export ≥5% of navigator openers, and ≥10% of unique shared visitors return within 30 days, with no performance/accessibility regression.
Kill: fewer than 5% navigator opens after at least 100 eligible treatment completions, zero attributable returns, or a privacy/performance/accessibility incident.

Conversion: chart → navigator → share/return → account → verified relevant product.

Scope: entry module, event taxonomy, safe share readback, accessibility/performance pass. No new celestial claims or paid product exposure.
