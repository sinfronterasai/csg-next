# R-015 — Privacy-First Career Chart Diagnostic (Resubmission)

Status: RESEARCH

Opportunity: A non-indexed career diagnostic for existing chart users, using CSG’s deterministic career facts before any public paid-product claim.

Evidence and gap: Existing results include career calculators from DesiUtils, Astromitra, Kaalveda, Vedic Rishi, AstroMool, and ZODIA. CSG can differentiate with Western/tropical calculations, deterministic facts, explicit known-time requirements, and privacy-forward handling. Vocation & Wealth Map remains UNKNOWN for public production status.

Cheapest experiment: 50/50 treatment/control test to eligible existing chart traffic for 30 days. Treatment receives an opt-in career diagnostic; control receives the current chart result. A 7-day instrumentation baseline precedes exposure.

Privacy contract: required birth inputs are processed only for the result; raw DOB, exact time, birthplace, and free-text feedback are not sent to unapproved third parties; retention/deletion behavior is explicit; analytics contain event IDs and aggregate properties, not raw birth data; consent and withdrawal are visible; feedback is optional and can be deleted.

Events and denominators: diagnostic impression/start/completion/result viewed/useful rating/waitlist interest; eligible starters are the completion denominator; unique user/session deduplication; qualified interest means explicit opt-in plus verified contact or authenticated account action.

Content guardrails: deterministic chart facts are separated from interpretive narrative; no employment, financial, or career guarantees; known-time limitations are visible.

Baseline: 7-day chart completion and career-interest signals.
Success: ≥25% diagnostic completion among treatment starters, ≥15% useful-rating submission, and ≥5% qualified interest after at least 100 treatment starters, with no privacy incident or chart-completion regression over 1 percentage point.
Kill: <10% completion after 100 starters, zero qualified interest, any privacy incident, or unsupported factual/guarantee claim.

Conversion: diagnostic → account/qualified waitlist → Vocation & Wealth Map only after production route, checkout, entitlement, and delivery are verified.

Scope: one fact pack, short result, consent/privacy implementation, feedback/waitlist events. No public SEO cluster or paid claim.
